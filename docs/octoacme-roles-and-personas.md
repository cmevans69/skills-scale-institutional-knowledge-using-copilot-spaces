# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, design test strategies, and ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release. They collaborate with developers to drive quality throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans and test cases
- Define quality acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Perform manual QA and coordinate automated testing
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness assessments
- Advocate for quality and testability in design discussions

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality and acceptance standards
- Reduce production defects and incidents
- Enable rapid, confident releases through comprehensive testing
- Drive a quality-first culture across the team

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Defect reports and issue discussions in sprint standups
- Release readiness checks and smoke test results
- Quality metrics and trend reports in stakeholder updates

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design decisions, and governance. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards while mentoring developers and managing technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and system design
- Review designs and code for scalability and maintainability
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor and support developers on technical challenges
- Ensure alignment with organizational standards and best practices
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs
- Participate in technical planning and estimation

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build team capability through mentorship and knowledge sharing
- Make sound architectural decisions that support business goals

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and technical guidance
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Architecture documentation and decision logs

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that projects meet security requirements, identify vulnerabilities, and embed security best practices throughout the development lifecycle. They work cross-functionally to reduce risk and maintain compliance.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Conduct security assessments and penetration testing
- Coordinate security scanning in CI/CD pipelines
- Advise on secure coding practices and frameworks
- Manage security incident response and post-mortems
- Ensure compliance with organizational and regulatory standards

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant features and releases
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and data breaches
- Build security awareness and practices across teams
- Enable rapid incident response and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement definitions in planning phase
- Security code review feedback and threat assessments
- Vulnerability reports and remediation plans
- Security incident communications and post-incident reviews

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers build, maintain, and improve deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release automation. They enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments while maintaining system stability and observability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and configuration
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Coordinate release activities and deployments
- Ensure rollback procedures and disaster recovery readiness
- Optimize deployment frequency and reliability
- Support incident response and troubleshooting

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment risk and incidents
- Improve system observability and mean-time-to-recovery
- Reduce manual effort and human error in releases

### Typical Communication
- Deployment pipeline and infrastructure discussions in planning
- Release coordination and deployment status in execution phase
- Incident alerts and runbooks during active issues
- Infrastructure and observability metrics in stakeholder reports

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, customer needs, and organizational priorities. They provide strategic direction, approve funding and resources, and ensure alignment with broader business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project scope, budget, and resources
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Communicate with executive leadership and external partners
- Escalate and resolve organizational blockers
- Validate solutions against business needs and market fit
- Participate in release reviews and go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align delivery with organizational strategy and priorities
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and engagement
- Enable rapid decision-making and issue escalation

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and business case reviews
- Milestone and release reviews with executive summaries
- Status updates and risk escalations to leadership
- Go/no-go decisions and business impact assessments

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design and UX Leads ensure that solutions are user-centered, intuitive, and aligned with brand standards. They conduct user research, create design artifacts, and guide the team in delivering excellent user experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create design specifications, wireframes, and prototypes
- Define user experience standards and accessibility requirements
- Review implementations for design fidelity and usability
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature prioritization
- Advocate for users and accessibility throughout delivery
- Document design decisions and design system updates

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, usable, and accessible features
- Maximize user satisfaction and engagement
- Reduce usability issues and rework
- Build consistent, recognizable user experiences

### Typical Communication
- Design briefs and user research findings during planning
- Design reviews and feedback during execution
- Usability testing results and design iterations
- Accessibility compliance and design system updates
