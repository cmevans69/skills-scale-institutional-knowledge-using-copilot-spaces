# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery approach with clear ownership, data-driven decision-making, and psychological safety. Our project management framework guides teams through initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.

### Core Principles
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named PM and Product Lead
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme employs a structured, lifecycle-based approach to project management that emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, and clear ownership. The organization runs projects through five distinct phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Close & Retrospective**. During initiation, teams validate business needs and align stakeholders around a lightweight Project One-pager that captures the problem statement, success metrics, and resource requirements. Once approved, the project moves into planning, where work is broken into shippable increments with prioritized backlogs, acceptance criteria, and a Definition of Done. This structured handoff ensures teams have clarity on scope and dependencies before development begins.

Execution and day-to-day delivery are driven by a consistent team rhythm: daily standups (15 minutes) focus on progress and blockers, weekly delivery syncs review milestones and flagged risks, and sprint planning pulls work that meets quality gates. Work flows through a project board with columns spanning Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. Pull requests are kept small (≤400 lines when possible), require at least one approval, and must pass automated tests and linting before merge. Quality is embedded throughout via unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and security scanning in CI, with manual QA applied to feature acceptance when needed.

OctoAcme's success relies on clear role definition and transparent communication. Three core roles drive projects: **Project Managers** coordinate delivery, manage risks and timelines, and ensure stakeholder alignment; **Product Managers** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure impact; and **Developers** implement features, collaborate on design, and contribute to planning and risk identification. This division of responsibility is supported by a cadence of weekly syncs between PM and Product Manager, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, monthly stakeholder updates, and a formal escalation path (Team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor) for risks and blockers. Additionally, OctoAcme prioritizes continuous improvement through post-sprint retrospectives and incident reviews, capturing learnings and converting them into actionable improvements tracked in the project backlog.
